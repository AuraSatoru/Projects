Giant Panda

The big bamboo-loving bears of the panda family.

Giant Panda

Family: Ursidae

Description: A large black-and-white bear with round ears and strong jaws.

Diet: Mostly bamboo, with some fruit, roots, and small animals.

Habitat: Mountain forests in central China.

Cool fact: Giant pandas can spend up to 14 hours a day eating.

Dwarf Panda

Family: Ursidae

Description: An extinct ancient relative of the giant panda, much smaller than today's panda.

Diet: It probably ate tough plants and bamboo.

Habitat: Ancient forests of southern China.

Cool fact: Fossil teeth show that it may be one of the oldest panda relatives.

Red Panda

Small, nimble tree climbers with beautifully striped tails.

Himalayan Red Panda

Family: Ailuridae

Description: The smaller red panda subspecies, with reddish fur and a white-edged face.

Diet: Bamboo, berries, acorns, insects, and eggs.

Habitat: Himalayan forests of Nepal, Bhutan, and northern India.

Cool fact: It has a special thumb-like wrist bone for holding bamboo.

Styan's Red Panda

Family: Ailuridae

Description: A red panda subspecies with darker fur and a larger body.

Diet: Bamboo, fruit, leaves, insects, and bird eggs.

Habitat: Mountain forests of southwestern China and northern Myanmar.

Cool fact: It is also called the Chinese red panda.
