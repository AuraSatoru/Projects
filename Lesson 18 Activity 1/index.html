Forest Section

Forests are large areas dominated by trees and other woody vegetation. They house a vast majority of the world's terrestrial biodiversity, including insects, birds, and mammals.

Ocean Section

Oceans cover more than 70% of the Earth's surface and are home to a diverse range of marine life, from tiny plankton to the largest animals on the planet.

Desert Section

Deserts are dry regions that receive very little precipitation. Despite their harsh conditions, deserts are home to unique and adapted wildlife.
