Eating Well and Staying Active

Eating healthy food fuels your body and brain for school, sports, and playtime. Choosing fresh fruits, crunchy vegetables, whole grains, and protein gives you steady energy throughout the day and keeps your immune system strong.

Drinking plenty of clean water and pairing balanced meals with daily physical activity helps build strong muscles and bones. Staying hydrated also keeps you alert in class and energized during playtime.

Quick Tips

  • Drink 6 to 8 glasses of water every day.
  • Fill half your plate with colorful fruits and vegetables.
  • Choose whole-fruit snacks over sugary packaged treats.
  • Get at least 60 minutes of active movement daily.
