Gojo Satoru

The Strongest Hero in the imaginative world of japan also known in the 'Jujutsu Kaisen' anime

Super Powers

  1. Infinity (Neutral): Creates an infinite space between Gojo and any attack.
  2. Cursed Technique Reversal: Red: The exact opposite of Blue, everything in its path.
  3. Hollow Technique: Purple: A combination of Blue and Red that creates an imaginary mass.
Weaknesses

  1. Emotional Ties: Memories of his past (like Suguru Geto) can cause him to hesitate.
  2. Civilians: He holds back his strongest attacks to avoid killing innocent bystanders.
  3. Overconfidence: His arrogance can lead him to let his guard down or underestimate a trap.
  4. Anti-Jujutsu Tools: Rare weapons like the Inverted Spear of Heaven can pierce his Infinity shield.
  5. Domain Amplification: Enemies can coat themselves in a domain aura to bypass his barrier and touch him.
